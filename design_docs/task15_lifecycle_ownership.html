Task 15 — lifecycle ownership and projection

This is the authoritative Task 15 inventory for bin/ab. Durable durable operation fields are written by the record lifecycle, command-local lifecycle fields remain process-only, projection fields are written only by operation_state_reset/ operation_state_publish, policy decision fields are produced by the decision layer, and report fields are command-local outputs. The projection is the only handoff consumed by operation-state output and command reports.

Ownership rules

  • operation_state_publish is the sole record/policy-to-projection conversion path. It is called after record loads and lifecycle updates before consumers run.
  • operation_state_record and command_report_emit consume projection fields. They do not parse the record, infer permissions from a status, or rewrite durable state.
  • operation_record_active is an ephemeral current-process flag: begin marks the current operation active, while loading an existing record leaves it inactive. Its projection copy lets report derivation preserve that distinction without treating the flag as persisted.
  • No approved exceptions are present. A future exception must name a written review finding and explicit human authorization in the table; prose alone is not an approval.
